# Redistribution terms

This repository includes computational inputs whose public redistribution terms
have not all been established. This document records the available evidence;
it grants no license and does not replace upstream terms.

No project-wide open-source license has been established for the project-written
solver, simulation, verification, documentation, or generated-result material.
The responsible rights holder must specify the applicable license before public
redistribution. The biological inputs are retained for reproducibility; their
presence does not imply permission to relicense them together.

The following source-specific questions also require resolution:

1. The pinned QL source files and Drosophila comparator snapshots contain no
   explicit license grant. A redistribution basis is also needed for the
   T-LGL template source.
2. The exact CD4 and macrophage Boolean exports were obtained through Biodivine
   Boolean Models (BBM)/Cell Collective. The pinned BBM README reserves model
   copyright to the respective authors and/or publishers. Its permission for
   other information is not a blanket license for model files. The biological
   articles have open-access licenses; those article notices alone do not
   establish the rights for every later encoding or adaptation.
3. Model expressions, truth tables and residual rows occur in derived JSON,
   BNET and certificate files as well as in source copies. Their redistribution
   scope must be considered together, with applicable notices and credits retained.

Pinned sources, hashes, model distinctions and licensing evidence are listed in
[THIRD_PARTY_SOURCES.md](docs/THIRD_PARTY_SOURCES.md). External Python and TeX
packages retain their own licenses. Licenses of unrelated Biodivine or Cell
Collective software do not establish the license of these model datasets.
