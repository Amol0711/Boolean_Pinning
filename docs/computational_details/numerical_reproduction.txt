NUMERICAL METHODS AND REPRODUCTION DETAILS

This guide records the fixed protocols, random-call order, budgets, uncertainty
calculations and verification scope of three studies: the 250,000-network
Bernoulli sample, the 18,000-network joint-rule sample and the separate
20,000-network J1 follow-up. Paths in each study section are relative to its
workflow: workflows/bernoulli/, workflows/joint_rules/ or workflows/j1_followup/.
The baseline J1 comparison cell has 2,000 rows and remains part of the joint-rule
sample; it is not pooled with the follow-up.

Use docs/REPRODUCTION.md and tools/reproduce.py from the repository root for
supported commands in isolated copies. Detailed direct commands below assume
such a copy. Source and current file hashes may differ where names or explanatory
metadata have been adapted. provenance/COMMITMENTS.json retains recorded
source/protocol/gate commitments; MANIFEST.json verifies distributed files.
Neither is permission to change seeds, rules, outcomes or unresolved bounds.

1. Shared model, solver and record conventions
---------------------------------------------
All three synthetic studies use synchronous Boolean updates, a zero target,
permanent zero-valued pins from time zero, every vertex eligible, unit costs,
and convergence from every compatible physical initial state. Self-loops and
repeated physical parents are retained. The three nonzero input corners are
B1=g(1,0), B2=g(0,1), B12=g(1,1); g(0,0)=0.

Nominal input slots remain distinct in the support graph and certificate tests.
G requires exactly one residual nominal slot in every residual row. G_prime
requires exactly one internal marked slot at every vertex of each cyclic SCC;
acyclic singleton rows need no such restriction. Repeated references read the
same physical bit in actual dynamics. Isolated component maps hold every
exterior deviation at zero. A constant-zero sentinel represents exterior slots
in the solver input. Acyclic singletons cost zero; marked copy cycles cost one.

Complex SCC optimization enumerates pin sets in increasing cardinality and
tests their entire compatible physical-state functional graph. Successful
pinning is not presumed monotone under adding pins. The production limits are
18 component coordinates, 18 pins, and 10,000,000 deterministic work units PER
complex SCC. One unit is one local-row evaluation in successor construction or
one controlled successor-edge traversal. At size 18, successor construction
alone costs 18*2^18=4,718,592 units. There is no wall-clock stopping rule.

A stopped search retains its proved lower bound, feasible upper witness and
specific stop reason. No draw is dropped, imputed, rerolled or given a solved-only
weight. Component search guards are distinct from independent-verifier guards.
The general SCC solver's standalone defaults are not the production limits of
these studies; the frozen study protocols supply the 18/18/10,000,000 settings.

Full-network SHA256 digests concatenate parent indices as little-endian signed
64-bit integers in row-major order, followed by the three corner entries per row
as unsigned bytes. Raw streams have one ordered record per draw. Detail streams
retain residual rows [original_vertex,parent_slot_0,parent_slot_1,B1,B2,B12].
Component nodes, pins and failure-state bits use the declared local indexing;
local_to_original translates them back to original physical vertex IDs and ends
with null for the zero sentinel. On the G fast path, component details can be
omitted while complete residual rows and the exact cycle count remain available.

Scientific compressed streams use deterministic ordering and gzip timestamps.
Writers close and check gzip trailers and record identities before publishing
completed streams/manifests. Byte comparisons concern declared scientific files;
runtime, machine information, run-start times and invocation records can differ.
Do not treat a partial directory without a completed manifest as a completed run.

3. Bernoulli study: sample identity and successive bounds
---------------------------------------------------------
There are five mixtures A--E and sizes n=100,400,1600,6400,25600, with 10,000
draws per cell. Deterministic consecutive type blocks each contain n/2 vertices:

  Mixture   First (output degree,q)   Second (output degree,q)   lambda
  A         (1,0.50)                  (3,0.10)                   0.4
  B         (1,0.10)                  (3,0.50)                   0.8
  C         (1,0.80)                  (3,0.20)                   0.7
  D         (1,0.35)                  (3,0.35)                   0.7
  E         (0,0.80)                  (4,0.30)                   0.6

Each network uses

  Generator(PCG64(SeedSequence([2026101301, mixture_id, n, replicate])))

where mixture_id is A=1,B=2,C=3,D=4,E=5 and replicate=0,...,9999. The first call
permutes the output-source-label multiset into an (n,2) row-major parent array.
The next calls rng.random((n,3)); entries are thresholded against the receiving
vertex's q in corner order (B1,B2,B12). The three corners are independent for this
Bernoulli study. A source-label permutation is the source projection of uniform
labeled-stub matching: each source sequence has product_i d_i! labeled preimages.
Worker count and task completion order do not change identities or records.

simulation/legacy/ contains the initial sample and deterministic bound-refinement computation. The
original residual-wide fallback used at most 12 residual coordinates and
2,000,000 work units, leaving 16,920 draws unresolved. Its separate post-run
double-row/copy-cycle refinement changed bounds without adding observations.
The later SCC computation regenerates those same 250,000 networks, preserves
their residuals, G status and every previously exact cost, and intersects SCC
bounds with preserved earlier bounds and the all-realization feasible interval,
retaining a witness for the smaller upper endpoint. Its caps were selected
after the original sampling and frozen before the SCC main replay.

The final record has 249,208 exact costs: 226,972 certified by G, another 10,798
by G_prime, and 11,438 resolved beyond G_prime. Of the 16,920 original unresolved
draws, 16,128 become exact and 792 remain intervals. Their 793 unresolved complex
SCCs exceed the 18-coordinate cap; there is no production work-budget stop.

The retained 10M timing pilot repeats the first 100 original replicates in each
of the 25 cells, totaling 2,500. All also appear in the main sample. Original,
refined, SCC and pilot records must not be pooled as independent observations.
The shipped pilot is simulation/results/pilot_10m/; references in documentation to other pilot calculations do not identify extra distributed data.

5. JointRules original joint-rule study: design and files
-----------------------------------------------
JointRules contains 18,000 original observations: J1,J2,J3, n=100,1600,25600, with 2,000
draws per cell. Its random namespace and records are separate from Bernoulli and the
later J1 follow-up. The complete rule is sampled categorically, not as a product
of singleton marginals. In code order c=B1+2*B2+4*B12, the names are:

  0 ZERO; 1 x1 AND NOT x2; 2 NOT x1 AND x2; 3 XOR;
  4 AND; 5 COPY1; 6 COPY2; 7 OR.

The exact weights in code order 0,...,7, divided by 100, are:

  J1, degree 1: (30,0,0,0,15,45,10,0)
  J1, degree 3: (60,0,0,0,10,10,20,0)
  J2, degree 2: (55,0,0,10,20,0,0,15)
  J3, degree 1: (30,0,0,0,0,45,15,10)
  J3, degree 3: (40,0,0,0,60,0,0,0)

J1 and J3 each have two consecutive n/2 type blocks; J2 has one degree-two
block. Their (lambda,rho) pairs are (29/80,19/40), (1/2,9/20), (1/5,5/8).
The fixed limiting means -log(1-lambda) are approximately 0.450201, 0.693147,
0.223144. No parameter is refitted to realized frequencies.

For family_id=1,2,3 and replicate=0,...,1999:

  Generator(PCG64(SeedSequence([2026101504, family_id, n, replicate])))

The first random call permutes the output-source-label multiset into ordered
receiver slots. In declared type-block order, rng.choice(8, size=type_count,
p=weights/100) then samples each vertex's complete code. Matching and rules are
independent, as are different vertices' rules. The protocol, source hashes and
independent exact-check gate were frozen before the main run.

All 18,000 costs are exact. G fails 139 times and G_prime 105 times, adding 34
certifications. All 106 complex SCC optima were independently checked; no
production stop or independent guard occurs. These are finite computational
checks, not proofs of the asymptotic theorem.

9. J1 independent coverage, frequency diagnostics and limitations
---------------------------------------------------------------
The independent validator imports no production functions. Its seeded replay
checks 20,000 networks, 512,000,000 coordinate rows, 40,000 degree-conditioned
rule-count vectors, every full-network digest and prescribed degree sequence,
16,008 residual rows, and 13,611 residual SCCs. The latter comprise 9,040 copy
cycles and 4,571 acyclic components. There are 14,948 repeated-parent rows in
full networks and none in nominal residuals. All 20,000 costs are exact; G holds
19,999 times and G_prime 20,000 times. The sole G failure has a three-coordinate
residual; three candidate pin sets and 16 compatible states independently
establish optimum one.

No noncopy cyclic SCC occurs in the new production sample and no independent
guard is reached. This absence is not an empirical stress test of difficult
SCC optimization. Independent guards remain 18 component coordinates, 100,000
candidate pin sets and 100,000,000 compatible states; direct whole-residual
optimization is limited to dimension 12. Guarded optima must not be described
as independently exhausted.

Before production, independent preflight exhausts all 1,024 ordered two-node,
two-slot zero-target networks, 4,096 pin sets and 9,216 compatible initial states;
it also checks seven edge fixtures, four bounded-exit fixtures and the empty
residual. The edge fixtures are constant zero, repeated-parent XOR cancellation,
repeated-parent AND, repeated-parent COPY1, nominally coupled independent
self-copies, a cooperative AND pair, and a three-cycle with zero exterior.
The bounded exits cover candidate count, compatible-state count, component size,
and witness-size guards. Empty residual has one candidate and one state.

A separate production preflight checks engine/solver agreement on the same
1,024 deterministic two-node networks and replays original largest-size J1
replicates 0,999,1999 without drawing any new production seed. A port check uses
100 original J1 draws at n=100, including noncopy cyclic components and repeated-
parent residuals. These old observations are validation inputs, not additions
to the 20,000 follow-up sample.

The independent arithmetic audit passes 372,115 checks. Independent and
production-side perturbation checks change diagnostic rule counts in memory
and confirm that law-derived references and complete cost summaries stay fixed.
Actual data files are unchanged. The detailed audit is qa/statistical_result.json;
the production invariance record is qa/production_no_refit.json.

Each degree class contains 256,000,000 categorical draws. All eight corner counts
are retained for every network/class, including zero-probability codes. The eight
positive degree-by-rule counts have prespecified Bonferroni simultaneous 95%
Clopper--Pearson intervals, each using coverage 1-0.05/8. Multinomial dependence
does not invalidate that bound. The two one-sided nonmonotone codes, XOR and OR
have zero counts in both classes. All design probabilities lie inside the eight
simultaneous intervals, but universal pointwise coverage is not claimed:
degree-one ZERO and AND exclude their design values in ordinary pointwise 95%
intervals. Pearson diagnostics are chi-square_3=9.450924 (p=0.023859) for degree
one, chi-square_3=2.869770 (p=0.412143) for degree three, and combined
chi-square_6=12.320694 (p=0.055185). These diagnostics do not gate acceptance or
authorize regeneration; complete counts/intervals are in
results/main/rule_frequency_summary.csv and qa/statistical_result.json.

Across all studies, computational lower/upper bounds are distinct from sampling
uncertainty. Moment intervals are approximate pointwise normal Monte Carlo
intervals for finite-size quantities; probability intervals are conservative
pointwise Clopper--Pearson envelopes, except for the explicitly simultaneous
eight-frequency diagnostic above. The Poisson quantities are fixed limiting
references, not exact finite-n null laws. No simultaneous coverage across all
study cells/bins/moments or finite-size approximation rate is asserted. The J1
follow-up was selected after inspection of the original discrepancy, so between-
batch contrasts are exploratory. It is one fixed-size study, not a size sweep,
convergence-rate estimate or general performance benchmark. Deterministic replay
checks implementation consistency; sampling intervals measure Monte Carlo
precision, and neither replaces the mathematical proofs.
