CD4 CONTEXTS: EXACT DECLARED BACKGROUNDS
All unlisted external inputs are0 in each new context. The four contexts are
not required to share identical cofactor backgrounds; comparisons are therefore
not interpreted as isolated causal effects of changing only one input.

APC: v_APC=1, v_MHC_II=1.
The released rules contain CD28=(B7 OR APC), TCR=(APC AND CD28),
CD3=TCR, and CD4=(TCR AND CD3 AND MHC_II). Thus MHC_II=1 is an explicit
coordinate condition enabling the CD4 branch; B7 remains0. MHC_II is part
of the environmental assignment, not an optimized internal intervention.

ECM: v_ECM=1.
The released integrin coordinate has Bintegrin=(TCR OR ECM), and
FAK_Tyr397 copies Bintegrin. No extra receptor coordinate is introduced.

IL2: v_IL2_e=1, v_CGC=1, v_IL2RB=1.
Both released IL2R and IL2R_HIGH formulas require CGC and IL2RB together
with either extracellular or endogenous IL2. IL2RA remains a dynamic
coordinate selecting the model's receptor branches. This cofactor background
is explicit, not claimed to be neutral: the released SHP2 formula also has
an IL2RB term without ligand gating. Table1 of the primary paper lists IL2,
CGC and IL2RB, but does not prescribe this particular complete Boolean assignment.

GalphaQ ligand: v_Galpha_QL=1.
The exact released input spelling maps to Galpha_Q=Galpha_QL and
PLCb=Galpha_Q. Other GPCR ligand inputs remain0.

The artificial CAV1_ACTIVATOR input remains0 in all four new CD4 contexts.
The source explains its role in artificial control of CAV1 activity; setting
this auxiliary input0 does not amount to knocking out the CAV1 scaffold,
which retains its other upstream logical inputs.

MACROPHAGE CONTEXTS AND THE EXTERNAL_ACTIVATOR INPUT
In every new macrophage context, v_External_Activator=1. Exactly one focal
input is also1: v_Bacteria, v_IFNG, or v_FASLG. Every other external input is0.

External_Activator is an auxiliary coordinate in this repository encoding,
not a named biological stimulus or a concentration described by Raza et al.
It directly supplies many receptor and signalling-component rows. Examples
checked in the frozen BNET include:
  IFNGR1 = External_Activator;
  IFNGR2 = External_Activator;
  JAK2 = External_Activator;
  FAS = External_Activator;
  ProCASP8 = External_Activator;
  CD40 = External_Activator;
  TNFRSF1A = External_Activator.
Fixing this coordinate1 is a declared technical background condition so
these model components are available. It is not claimed to reproduce a
physiological resting state, protein concentration, or measured expression
profile. Fixing it0 would remove those rows' activity by construction;
that different convention is retained transparently in the zero endpoint.

IFNG maps to the IFNGR rule together with IFNGR1, IFNGR2 and JAK2;
STAT1 copies IFNGR. FASLG maps into the FASLG_FAS complex through the
FAS receptor. These connections justify the focal input names, without
assuming a final transcriptional or apoptotic phenotype in advance.

Bacteria is an aggregate repository input. LPS, Flagellin, Diacyl_Lipopeptides
and Triacyl_Lipopeptides each copy it; Viral_Bacterial_CpG is Virus OR Bacteria.
Consequently, the context must be called a Bacteria-input condition, not a
pure LPS or a specified experimental bacterial infection condition.

Direct caspase activators, Mitochondrial_Activator, IRF activators and
RELA_NFKB1_Activator remain0 in all three new contexts. No direct downstream
activation is added after outcomes are observed.

PRESPECIFICATION AND REPORTING
Both all-zero/all-one endpoints for each model are retained and identified as
conditions observed before focal-context selection. The seven focal assignments,
target-discovery rule, eligibility, computational budgets and unresolved statuses
were specified before their computations. The CD4_APC and MAC_BACTERIA figure
cases were selected before their computations and retained regardless of outcome.

Do not infer a healthy/disease phenotype from input fractions or target bits.
Do not claim all model coordinates are genes. Treat input assignments as an
environmental baseline and report internal/model-node intervention cost separately
from the cost convention that charges identity-completed external inputs.
Orbit discovery failure does not establish that no fixed point exists. A noncopy residual
or an exhausted search budget does not establish intervention failure. Retain every
prespecified case with its appropriate verified or unresolved status.
